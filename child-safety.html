StickHub Child Safety Standards

StickHub has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse material (CSAM), child exploitation, or any content that promotes harm toward minors.

Our Standards

  • Users may not upload, share, request, or promote child sexual abuse material.
  • Any account found violating this policy will be permanently banned.
  • Illegal content will be reported to the appropriate authorities when required.
  • Users can report inappropriate content directly through StickHub support.
  • We cooperate with law enforcement requests where legally required.

Reporting

To report content or users violating these standards, contact:

Email: support@stickhub.app

Enforcement

Violations may result in immediate account suspension or permanent removal without warning.
