Delete Your StickHub Account

You may request deletion of your StickHub account and associated app data.

How to Request Deletion

Email stickhubapp@gmail.com from the email address linked to your StickHub account.

Please include: “Delete my StickHub account” in the subject line.

What May Be Deleted

  • Account profile information
  • Humidor entries
  • Journal notes and ratings
  • Uploaded cigar images
  • Community posts tied to your account, where technically feasible

What May Be Retained

Some information may be retained for legal, security, fraud prevention, abuse prevention, backup, or operational reasons. Data stored in backups may take additional time to be removed through normal backup rotation.

Timing

We will review deletion requests and process them within a reasonable timeframe.
